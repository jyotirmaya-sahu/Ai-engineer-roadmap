# Interview-Ready (Dubai)

**The one-line idea:** This is the last mile — turning everything you've learned into *offers*, by drilling the exact questions Dubai AI Engineer interviews ask, practising the human parts (live coding, your career-gap story), and shipping a portfolio + applications that actually get replies.

## Why we even need it

You can know RAG, agents, and LLMOps cold and still not get hired. Interviews are a **separate skill** from building. An interviewer isn't grading whether you *can* build a RAG system — they're grading whether, in 45 minutes and under pressure, you can:

1. **Explain a design out loud** — draw the boxes, name the trade-offs, put numbers on latency and cost.
2. **Defend decisions** — "why hybrid search and not just vectors?" "what breaks at 10× traffic?"
3. **Tell your story** — including a ~1-year gap — in a way that reads as *strength*, not apology.
4. **Prove it's real** — a GitHub with clean READMEs a hiring manager can skim in 90 seconds.

Every skill on this roadmap was input. This node is **output**: the conversion step from knowledge to a signed contract in Dubai.

## Why it matters for an AI Engineer (especially in Dubai)

Dubai's AI hiring in 2026 is dominated by **"AI Engineer" = applied GenAI + deployment** roles: RAG, agents, LLM APIs, and getting them to production on a cloud. The demand data from real Dubai job descriptions is blunt — **deployment shows up in ~82% of postings, Python ~77%, cloud ~40%, MLOps ~37%, GenAI/LLM ~36%** (with RAG ~27%, agents ~24%). So the drills below aren't generic ML-interview prep; they're aimed at *exactly* what a Dubai AI Engineer loop tests.

There's also a Dubai-specific twist: many roles are **contract or relocation-dependent**, interviewers often care about **cost discipline** (teams run lean), and you'll frequently interview with a **non-specialist hiring manager** who needs you to explain clearly. That rewards the "trade-off defense" and "cost/latency estimation" drills disproportionately.

## Real world: how this plays out

> A candidate relocating to Dubai after a health-related gap had built a solid RAG contract-assistant. In mock loops she kept *knowing* the answer but freezing when asked "why 500-token chunks and not 1000?" — she had the intuition, not the *sentence*. Two weeks of trade-off-defense and cost-estimation drills gave her a crisp 20-second answer for every decision in her project. Same project, same skills — she now sounded like someone who'd shipped it at work. She stopped getting rejected at the design round.

## Interview tip
Treat the interview as a product you're shipping: your **portfolio is the landing page**, your **project deep-dive is the demo**, your **trade-off answers are the objection-handling**, and your **career-gap story is the trust-builder**. Rehearse each until it's a 20–60 second reflex, not an improvisation. Interviewers hire the person who sounds like they've *already done the job* — these drills manufacture exactly that signal.
